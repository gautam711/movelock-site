Delete your MoveLock data

This page is for the MoveLock app on Google Play (package com.movelock.app), published by the developer of MoveLock. MoveLock has no accounts, so most of your data never leaves your phone. Here is how to delete each kind.

1. Data stored on your phone (settings, limited apps, history, streaks, badges)

Delete it yourself, at once, in one of two ways:

  • Uninstall MoveLock, or
  • Android Settings > Apps > MoveLock > Storage > Clear storage.

Nothing of this data is kept anywhere else; there is no backup on our side. The camera never records or uploads, so there are no images or videos to delete.

2. Anonymous analytics events (PostHog)

If analytics is on, the app sends anonymous usage events tied to a random id, never to your name or email. To stop them, turn off Settings > Analytics in the app. To delete the events already sent: open Settings > Privacy, tap Copy id under "Your anonymous analytics id", and email it to golustudios66@gmail.com with the subject "MoveLock data deletion". We delete all events for that id within 30 days and confirm by email. Without the id we cannot find your events, because nothing else links them to you.

3. Purchase records (Google Play and RevenueCat)

If you bought MoveLock Pro, Google Play keeps the order under your Google account, and our subscription provider RevenueCat keeps the purchase token and an anonymous app user id so that Pro can be restored. Email us from the address above to have the RevenueCat record deleted; note that deleting it also removes the ability to restore Pro on a new phone. Google Play's own order history is managed by Google under your Google account.

4. Advertising (free plan only)

Ads on the results screen are served by Google AdMob using the Android advertising id. Reset or delete that id in Android Settings > Privacy > Ads. We hold no copy of it.

What is kept

After a deletion request we keep nothing about you, except what Google Play keeps for your purchases under Google's own policies and, if applicable, invoices we must retain for tax law. Questions: golustudios66@gmail.com. See also the privacy policy.
