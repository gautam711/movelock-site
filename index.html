Move to unlock.

MoveLock limits the apps you choose. When your session runs out, a short set of squats or push-ups, counted by your phone's camera, unlocks them again. The camera never records or uploads.

Get it on Google Play

How it works

  1. Pick up to 3 apps on the free plan (unlimited on Pro) and a session length.
  2. A countdown pill warns you before time is up; then the apps lock.
  3. Do the exercise in front of the phone. Counting happens on the device.
  4. You are back. Streaks, badges and weekly reports keep you going.
