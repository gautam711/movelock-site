MoveLock Privacy Policy

Effective date: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what the MoveLock app ("MoveLock", "the app") does with information. MoveLock is provided by Gautam Nagvekar ("we", "us"), Naner Wada, Pernem, Goa 403512, India. Contact: golustudios66@gmail.com.

Short version:

  • The camera picture is analysed on your phone to count reps. It is never recorded, stored or uploaded, and the body points (pose landmarks) computed from it never leave the phone.
  • There is no account and no sign-up, and we run no server of our own. Your chosen apps, the time you spend in them, your streaks and stats stay on your phone.
  • Three service providers receive limited data: Google AdMob (ads on the free plan), PostHog (anonymous usage analytics that you can turn off in Settings) and RevenueCat (purchase status for MoveLock Pro). Purchases themselves are handled by Google Play or the Apple App Store.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • MoveLock is not directed at children under 13.

1. Who is responsible

Gautam Nagvekar is the controller of the limited personal data described in section 3. Questions, requests and complaints: golustudios66@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your phone

MoveLock works mostly offline. The following is created and kept only on your device; we never receive it:

  • Camera frames. While you exercise, the camera picture is processed in memory by on-device pose detection (Google MediaPipe on Android, Apple's Vision framework on iOS) to count repetitions and hold time. Frames are never saved, never recorded as video and never sent anywhere. The pose landmarks (body point positions) computed from them are used for counting and then discarded; they are never sent anywhere and never included in analytics.
  • The apps you choose and their usage time. On Android, MoveLock uses the Usage access permission to see which app is in the foreground so it can count session time only in the apps you chose. On iOS, the apps you choose are opaque tokens from Apple's Screen Time framework; MoveLock does not see their names. This information is never sent off the device.
  • Your settings, sessions, exercise history, streaks, stats, weekly report and "not counted" log.
  • Local reminders (the weekly report and optional idle reminder) are scheduled on the phone; there is no push-notification server.
  • The share card image is created on the phone. It contains your exercise, reps, streak and time reclaimed, never app names or a photo, and it leaves the phone only if you choose a destination in your system share sheet. What the receiving app does with it is governed by that app's policy.

Uninstalling MoveLock deletes all of this. On Android you can also clear it in Settings > Apps > MoveLock > Storage > Clear storage.

3. Information that leaves your phone

All of the traffic below is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). None of it is linked to your name, email or any account, because MoveLock has no accounts and never identifies you to these providers.

3.1 Purchases (Google Play, Apple App Store)

If you buy MoveLock Pro, the payment is processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store under their own terms and privacy policies. We never see your payment card details.

3.2 Advertising: Google AdMob (free plan only)

The free plan shows at most three ads a day, only on the results screen after you finish an exercise and never during one. To serve and measure these ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects:

  • device identifiers: the advertising ID on Android, and the IDFA on iOS only if you allow tracking in Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, plus SDK instance and app-set identifiers;
  • approximate (coarse) location derived from your IP address;
  • ad interactions (ads viewed and tapped) and diagnostic / performance information.

Google may use this data, and may make it available to advertising partners through its ad auctions, to show ads, measure them, prevent fraud and, where you allow it, personalise ads. Before the first ad, Google's consent tool (UMP) asks for your choices where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and some US states); on iOS the tracking prompt follows. If you decline, you get non-personalised ads. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings at any time. MoveLock Pro has no ads, so AdMob collects nothing for Pro users. Google's own privacy policy and its page on how Google uses information from apps that use its services describe Google's processing.

3.3 Analytics: PostHog (can be turned off)

To understand which features help, release builds send anonymous usage events to PostHog: for example that an exercise was completed (exercise type, target and achieved reps or seconds), that the paywall was shown or closed, that an ad was shown or skipped, or that a reminder setting changed. Events carry a random anonymous ID created by the SDK. They never contain app names, package names, pose landmarks, camera data or free text. PostHog is configured to discard your IP address, create no person profiles and record no sessions. Events are stored in PostHog's United States region for up to 12 months.

While analytics is on, Settings > Privacy shows this anonymous ID ("Your anonymous analytics id"); it is not shown while analytics is off. If you want your analytics data deleted, you may quote that ID in your email to golustudios66@gmail.com so we can find your events. We never link this ID to your name or email address.

Opt out: Settings > Analytics opt-out stops all analytics immediately.

3.4 Subscription status: RevenueCat

RevenueCat manages MoveLock Pro purchases for us. It receives your store purchase history and receipts (product, date, renewal status) together with a random anonymous app user ID, and tells the app whether Pro is active. It receives no name or email from us.

3.5 What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, videos, audio, files, precise location, health records, browsing history, messages or the list of apps on your phone. We do not use crash-reporting or session-recording tools.

4. Why we use this information (legal bases)

  • Providing the app and Pro (RevenueCat, store billing): performance of our contract with you.
  • Ads on the free plan (AdMob): your consent where the law requires it (collected by Google's consent tool), otherwise our legitimate interest in funding a free version.
  • Analytics (PostHog): our legitimate interest in improving the app, with an in-app opt-out; or your consent where the law requires it.
  • Security and fraud prevention (AdMob, RevenueCat): legitimate interest and legal obligations.

5. Sharing and selling

We do not sell your personal information and we do not share it for money. We share data only with the service providers named in section 3, who process it to provide their service, and with authorities when the law requires it. AdMob's personalised advertising may count as "sharing" or "targeted advertising" under some US state laws; you can opt out through Google's consent message, by declining tracking on iOS, by resetting or deleting your advertising ID, or by using MoveLock Pro.

6. International transfers

Our service providers may process data in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers rely on the providers' standard contractual clauses or equivalent safeguards.

  • Decline personalised ads in Google's consent message, decline tracking on iOS, or reset / delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Delete on-device data by uninstalling the app (Android: or Clear storage).
  • Cancel a subscription in Google Play or in your Apple Account settings (deleting the app does not cancel it).
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR or US state laws) you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority. Email golustudios66@gmail.com. Because we hold no account and the providers' data is not linked to your identity, we may not be able to find data that belongs to you; we will tell you what we can do and forward deletion requests to our providers where they can act on them.

We answer requests within the period the law requires (usually one month) and do not discriminate against you for using your rights.

8. Retention

On-device data stays until you delete it or uninstall the app. Analytics events are kept for up to 12 months. Purchase records are kept by RevenueCat and the stores as long as needed for your subscription and for legal and tax obligations. AdMob data is kept according to Google's policies.

9. Children

MoveLock is not directed at children under 13 (or the higher minimum age where you live) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the app and data was collected, email golustudios66@gmail.com and we will help delete what we can.

10. Security

We keep data on your device wherever possible, use HTTPS for all SDK traffic, and keep personal data out of analytics events. No method is perfectly secure.
